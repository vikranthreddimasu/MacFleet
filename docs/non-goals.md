# Non-Goals

The next prototype should not become a full catering management suite.

## Explicit Non-Goals

- Invoicing.
- Payments.
- Deposits.
- Menu pricing.
- CRM.
- Customer portal.
- Full inventory management.
- Procurement.
- Payroll.
- Full staff scheduling.
- Real AI provider integration.
- Autonomous event management.

## Product Non-Goals

- Do not recreate Fusion feature-by-feature.
- Do not center the app around chat.
- Do not make "risk review" the primary product surface.
- Do not make AI feel like the boss.
- Do not hide source details behind summaries.

## Technical Non-Goals

- No database migration is required for the prototype.
- No auth system is required for the prototype.
- No deployment pipeline is required for the prototype.
- No dependency-heavy frontend stack is required until the app direction is
  validated.
