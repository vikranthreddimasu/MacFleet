# Product Brief: Catering Ops

## North Star

Catering Ops is a familiar catering event book that quietly carries the
memory, risk-sensing, and handoff burden.

The product should feel like an operations system that catering teams already
understand: events, orders, service schedules, BEO-style event sheets,
production notes, setup notes, delivery notes, checklists, status tracking,
and handoff. Intelligence lives inside those workflows instead of replacing
them.

## Users

- Catering managers who need the day's event picture.
- Coordinators who maintain event records and client details.
- Kitchen leads who need production, packing, and dietary clarity.
- Setup and delivery leads who need load times, room details, access notes,
  equipment, and blockers.
- Institutional food-service staff who need a low-drama system they can trust.

## Product Promise

The product helps teams answer:

- What is going out today?
- Which events are ready, blocked, or at risk?
- What changed, and who needs to know?
- What needs to be prepped, packed, delivered, set up, served, cleaned up, or
  followed up?
- What should be included in shift handoff?

## Product Stance

AI carries complexity, not authority. It can summarize, detect missing
information, trace change impact, suggest tasks, and draft handoff. Humans
approve operational changes and remain accountable for decisions.

## Adoption Principle

Do not make users learn an "AI command center" before they can do catering
work. Start with Today, Events, Event Detail, Production, Setup and Delivery,
Tasks, Review, and Handoff.
